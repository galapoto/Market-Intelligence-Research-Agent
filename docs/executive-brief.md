# Executive intelligence brief — Nordic EV charging software

**Research date:** 3 November 2026. **Decision horizon:** a hypothetical 2027 B2B pilot. **Audience:** business leadership.

## Decision and recommendation
A **Denmark-first customer-discovery pilot**, retaining Sweden as a strong alternative, is a **conditional recommendation**, not an established market fact. The scoring framework rated Sweden **21/25** and Denmark **20/25**; Denmark's suggested priority depends on timing, infrastructure growth and the constraints of a small entrant. It could reverse with different assumptions or budget.

## Findings with evidence boundaries
1. **AFIR regulatory requirements create operational work (HIGH for legal text; MEDIUM for claimed software-switching effect).** Verify obligations in [EU Regulation 2023/1804](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1804/oj). The regulation does not explicitly require **OCPP 2.0.1**; a possible migration effect is an inference.
2. **Charging infrastructure expanded rapidly (HIGH for transcribed historical IEA indicators, pending source-file revalidation).** Denmark fast chargers: 1,200 (2022) to 3,000 (2023), **+150%**. Sweden slow chargers: 15,000 to 30,000, **+100%**. These are **2022–2023**, not 2026 live counts.
3. **B2B charging software has multiple established competitors (MEDIUM).** A market-fragmentation thesis requires vendor-by-vendor and pricing verification; market sizing from a single consultancy is not a bankable forecast.
4. **Niche demand among smaller operators may exist (MEDIUM/LOW).** No verified Nordic primary interviews show willingness to pay or reliable acquisition economics.
5. **Operator consolidation might create vendor reassessment windows (MEDIUM).** Specific M&A events require source verification; subsequent software replacements are predictions, not demonstrated outcomes.

## Required next decisions
**Before a build commitment:** interview 10–15 operators in Denmark/Sweden; quantify operational pain points, current software stack, switching constraints, price tolerance and regulatory responsibility. Benchmark competitors and document the difference between AFIR compliance and optional OCPP versions.

**Decision gate:** progress to an MVP only after evidence of willingness to pay, obtainable pilot partners, implementation feasibility and reasonable unit economics.

## What this analysis does NOT show
- No proof that a viable software business exists.
- No verified Nordic customer price sensitivity or software switching rate.
- No proof that Denmark is objectively superior to Sweden.
- No forecast derived directly from the 2010–2023 dataset.
